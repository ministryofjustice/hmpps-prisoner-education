Right to work in the UK

All British and Irish citizens have the right to work in the UK.

Non-British and Irish citizens have the right to work if they have:

  • valid settled status in the UK
  • a work visa
  • indefinite leave to remain or enter (ILR or ILE) in the UK
  • right of abode in the UK
